Community Guidelines

{{ settings.brand_name }} is for adults looking to make genuine connections. These guidelines keep it respectful and safe.

Be yourself

  • Use recent photos of you. No celebrities, cartoons or other people.
  • Be honest about your age, intentions and relationship status.
  • One account per person.

Be respectful

  • No harassment, threats, bullying or hate speech — including on the basis of caste, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or ethnicity.
  • No unsolicited sexual content. No sharing others' private images.
  • "No" means no. Stop contacting someone who isn't interested.

Keep it safe

  • Never ask for or send money, gift cards or crypto.
  • Don't post phone numbers, emails or social handles on your profile — use private number sharing with a match instead.
  • Anyone under 18 is not allowed. Report anyone you think is underage.

No commercial use

  • No advertising, promotion, recruitment or solicitation.

Enforcement

We may remove content, issue warnings, or suspend or remove accounts. Serious violations, including anything involving minors or credible threats, may be reported to authorities. You can appeal a suspension.
