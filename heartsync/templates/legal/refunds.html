Subscriptions, Cancellation & Refunds

Renewal

Paid plans renew automatically each month at the then-current price until you cancel. We'll show the price and renewal date before you pay and on your Billing page.

Cancelling

Cancel anytime from Billing → Cancel subscription. You won't be charged again, and premium features stay active until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Refunds

  • If you were charged in error or charged after cancelling, contact {{ settings.support_email }} and we'll refund the charge.
  • If your account is removed for breaching our terms, unused time is not refunded except where required by law.
  • Other refund requests are considered case by case and as required by applicable consumer protection law. [Counsel to finalise.]

Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails we'll notify you and retry. Premium features may be paused until payment succeeds.
